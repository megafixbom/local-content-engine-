To grow the Tiny Riffs Guitar channel, we need to create engaging content that appeals to both young kids and their parents. Here is a 2-week content calendar with fresh, different ideas:

**Week 1:**
- Shorts: 
  1. "Guitar Surprises for Kids" - Show fun, unexpected sounds kids can make on the guitar to spark curiosity and attract parents looking for unique activities.
  2. "Kid's Guitar Goal Setting" - Encourage kids to set small, achievable goals for their guitar practice, attracting parents who value goal-oriented learning.
  3. "Guitar Fun in Mumbai Parks" - Film a short guitar lesson or practice in a Mumbai park, highlighting the convenience and beauty of learning guitar in local, outdoor settings.
  4. "Why Kids Love Guitar" - Share testimonials or short interviews with young guitar students about why they enjoy playing, to reassure parents about the benefits of guitar lessons.
  5. "Guitar for Beginners: First Steps" - Provide a brief, easy-to-follow introduction to the first steps in learning guitar, attracting new students and parents looking for a starting point.
- Lesson: "Introduction to Guitar Strings" - Teach kids the basics about guitar strings, how to identify them, and basic string care, in simple steps, to educate and reassure parents about the quality of lessons.

**Week 2:**
- Shorts: 
  1. "Mumbai Kids' Guitar Challenges" - Create a fun, light-hearted challenge for kids to practice a specific guitar skill, encouraging engagement and practice.
  2. "Guitar Lessons at Home" - Showcase the convenience and comfort of having guitar lessons at home, appealing to parents who prioritize convenience and safety.
  3. "Guitar and Movement" - Combine guitar playing with fun movements or dances, attracting kids who enjoy interactive and physical activities.
  4. "Guitar for Kids: Choosing the Right Size" - Offer advice on selecting the appropriate guitar size for kids, helping parents make informed decisions about their child's guitar journey.
  5. "Meet a Guitar Mentor" - Introduce a guest guitar teacher or mentor who can inspire kids with their story and playing, providing diversity and new perspectives.
- Lesson: "Learning Your First Guitar Song" - Guide kids through learning their first simple song on the guitar, using easy-to-follow steps and practice tips, to motivate and satisfy the desire for quick progress.

For the best posting rhythm, consider posting one short video per day and one lesson video per week. This consistent schedule helps maintain viewer engagement and anticipation for new content. Posting times should ideally be when the target audience (parents and kids) is most active online, such as early evening or late afternoon.
