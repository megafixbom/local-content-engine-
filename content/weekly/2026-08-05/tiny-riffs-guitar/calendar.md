To grow the Tiny Riffs Guitar channel, focusing on engaging, educational, and entertaining content is key. Here's a 2-week content calendar designed to attract parents and kids alike:

### Week 1
- **Short 1: "Guitar for Little Hands"** - Introduce a simple guitar exercise for small hands. Attracts parents who worry about their child's ability to play due to small hand size.
- **Short 2: "Mumbai's Guitar Story Time"** - Share a local story related to music and guitars. Appeals to parents looking for content that connects their children to the community.
- **Short 3: "Guitar Sound Effects"** - Teach kids to make fun sound effects using the guitar. Engages kids with the creative potential of the instrument.
- **Short 4: "Guitar Heroes for Kids"** - Introduce kids to a young or inspiring guitarist. Inspires kids with role models who started playing at a young age.
- **Short 5: "Setting Up for Success"** - Offer tips on how to set up a practice space at home. Helps parents create an environment conducive to their child's musical development.
- **Lesson: "From Strum to Song"** - Teach the basic steps to go from a single strum to playing a simple song. Encourages kids and parents by showing tangible progress in a short timeframe.

### Week 2
- **Short 1: "Guitar Games for Practice"** - Showcase a fun game that makes guitar practice enjoyable. Attracts parents looking for ways to keep their kids engaged and motivated.
- **Short 2: "Meet a Young Guitarist"** - Interview a young guitarist from Mumbai about their journey. Inspires kids with real-life examples of peers who play the guitar.
- **Short 3: "Guitar Care 101"** - Teach kids the basics of guitar maintenance. Empowers kids to take care of their instrument and appreciate its value.
- **Short 4: "Local Guitar Events"** - Inform viewers about upcoming music events in Mumbai that kids might enjoy. Keeps parents informed about opportunities for their kids to experience live music.
- **Short 5: "Overcoming Frustration"** - Offer advice on how to deal with frustration while learning to play. Reassures parents and kids that setbacks are part of the learning process.
- **Lesson: "Playing with Feel"** - Teach kids how to add emotion and feeling to their playing. Helps kids and parents understand the expressive side of music, enhancing their connection to the instrument.

The best posting rhythm for a small channel is to post content consistently, ideally 3 times a week, mixing between Shorts and lesson videos. This allows for a steady stream of content without overwhelming the audience. Engaging with the comments and creating a community around the channel is also crucial for growth.
