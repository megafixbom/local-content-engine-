### Script 1: "Guitar Magic for Little Hands"
- Hook: "Hey there, young rockstars! Want to make your guitar sing?"
- Body: "Let's learn a cool guitar trick called 'string sliding'. First, place your finger on the third fret of the low E string. Next, slide your finger down to the first fret without picking the string again. Finally, pick the string to hear the sliding sound! Repeat this a few times to get a feel for it."
- Call to Action: "Try it out and show me your sliding skills in the comments below! Don't forget to subscribe to Tiny Riffs Guitar for more fun guitar lessons for kids, and if you're in Mumbai, let's jam together!"

### Script 2: "Play 'Happy Birthday' Like a Pro!"

- Body: "Let's break it down into simple steps. First, tune your guitar. Next, learn the basic melody notes: C, C, G, G, A, G, F, G, C, C. Place your fingers on these notes one by one as you play them. Remember, practice makes perfect!"
- Call to Action: "Practice 'Happy Birthday' and play it for someone special. Share your experience or a video of you playing in the comments! For personalized lessons, reach out to me at Tiny Riffs Guitar, your friendly guitar teacher in Malad, Mumbai."

### Script 3: "First Funky Riff"
- Hook: "Are you ready to groove with your first funky guitar riff?"
- Body: "Let's start with a simple down-up strumming pattern on the open E string. Strum down, then up, then down again. Now, let's add some funk by muting the string with your palm after each strum. Practice this pattern a few times to get the hang of it!"
