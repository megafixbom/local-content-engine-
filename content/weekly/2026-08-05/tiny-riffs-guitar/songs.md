Here are five original, kid-friendly song ideas for the Tiny Riffs Guitar channel:

1. **Title:** Happy Vibes Only
**Theme:** Spreading positivity and good feelings
**Chord Progression:** G - Em - C - D
**Structure:** Intro/Verse/Chorus
**Lyrical Hook:** "Smile and the world smiles with you"
**Why kids will love it:** Who doesn't love feeling happy and positive? This song will uplift kids and encourage them to spread joy.

2. **Title:** Little Monsters Boogie
**Theme:** Imaginary creatures having fun
**Chord Progression:** C - G - Am - F
**Structure:** Intro/Verse/Chorus/Bridge
**Lyrical Hook:** "My little monster friends are dancing tonight"
**Why kids will love it:** Kids love imagination and fantasy, and this song will let them explore their creativity and have a blast.

3. **Title:** Treasure Hunt Adventure
**Theme:** Going on a treasure hunt
**Chord Progression:** D - A - Bm - G
**Structure:** Intro/Verse/Chorus
**Lyrical Hook:** "X marks the spot, let's dig and see"
**Why kids will love it:** Who doesn't love a good adventure? This song will spark kids' sense of curiosity and excitement.

4. **Title:** My Favorite Things
**Theme:** Appreciating the little things in life
**Chord Progression:** A - D - E - G
**Structure:** Intro/Verse/Chorus
**Lyrical Hook:** "I love playing guitar on a sunny day"
**Why kids will love it:** This song will help kids focus on the positive aspects of their lives and appreciate the small joys.

5. **Title:** Dream Big Sky
**Theme:** Reaching for your dreams and aspirations
**Chord Progression:** E - B7 - C#m - A
**Structure:** Intro/Verse/Chorus
**Lyrical Hook:** "My dreams are flying high, up in the sky"
**Why kids will love it:** This song will inspire kids to aim high and believe in themselves, promoting confidence and self-esteem.
