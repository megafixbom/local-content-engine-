For a small channel, the best posting rhythm is to post 2-3 times a week, with a mix of Shorts and lesson videos. This will help maintain consistency and keep the audience engaged.

Week 1:
- Shorts: "Guitar Superhero" - Introduce a fun guitar-playing superhero to inspire kids. This will attract kids who love superheroes and adventure.
- Shorts: "Tiny Fingers, Big Sound" - Show how small hands can play the guitar. This will reassure parents that their child can learn to play despite their small hands.
- Shorts: "Mumbai's Youngest Guitarist" - Feature a young student from Mumbai playing the guitar. This will attract local parents who want to see kids from their area learning guitar.
- Shorts: "Guitar Feelings" - Ask kids to express their emotions through guitar playing. This will attract kids who love to express themselves creatively.
- Shorts: "Guitar Buddy System" - Introduce a buddy system for kids to practice guitar together. This will attract parents who want their child to have a study buddy or friend to practice with.
- Lesson: "Let's Play a Happy Song" - Teach kids to play a simple happy song on the guitar. This will attract parents who want their child to learn fun, upbeat songs.

Week 2:
- Shorts: "Guitar Scavenger Hunt" - Create a scavenger hunt to find different parts of the guitar. This will attract kids who love solving puzzles and playing games.
- Shorts: "Malad's Guitar Hotspot" - Showcase the convenience of learning guitar in Malad, Mumbai. This will attract local parents who want a teacher close to home.
- Shorts: "Guitar Magic Tricks" - Teach kids simple guitar tricks to amaze their friends. This will attract kids who love magic and want to impress their friends.
- Shorts: "Guitar Journal" - Encourage kids to keep a journal of their guitar practice. This will attract parents who want their child to develop a habit of regular practice and reflection.
- Shorts: "Guitar Sharing" - Encourage kids to share their guitar playing with family and friends. This will attract parents who want their child to build confidence in performing in front of others.
- Lesson: "My Favorite Guitar Chord" - Teach kids to play their favorite chord on the guitar. This will attract parents who want their child to learn their favorite songs and chords.
